About the Laboratory

The TSU Meat Science Laboratory integrates analytical chemistry, spatial nutrition analysis, and consumer sensory innovation to advance scientific understanding of meat quality, safety, and nutritional integrity. Our research leverages MALDI–MSI, LC–MS/MS, and interdisciplinary collaboration across institutions including Vanderbilt University and the University of Wisconsin–Madison.

Research Themes

  • Spatial chemistry and MALDI imaging of variety meats
  • Nutritional integrity and metabolomic profiling
  • Meat quality, safety, and processing innovation
  • Consumer sensory evaluation and product development
