Laboratory News & Updates

Recent achievements, awards, presentations, outreach activities, and announcements from the TSU Meat Science Laboratory.

September 2026

TSU Meat Science Laboratory Participates in Tennessee State Fair

Lab members engaged with consumers, students, and industry stakeholders during the Tennessee State Fair in Lebanon, Tennessee. The team showcased research activities related to meat science, food quality, and analytical chemistry.

August 2026

Research Presented at IFT Annual Meeting

Members of the laboratory presented research findings at the Institute of Food Technologists (IFT) Annual Meeting in Chicago, Illinois, highlighting ongoing projects in metabolomics, spatial chemistry, and consumer food science.

July 2026

New Undergraduate Researchers Join the Lab

The laboratory welcomed several undergraduate researchers interested in artificial intelligence applications, food safety, meat quality, and analytical chemistry.

June 2026

Collaboration Visit with UW–Madison Researchers

The TSU Meat Science Laboratory expanded collaborative efforts through discussions with researchers from the University of Wisconsin–Madison to explore advanced analytical techniques and multidisciplinary research opportunities.

May 2026

Graduate Student Research Milestone

Graduate researchers completed major experimental work involving mass spectrometry, food chemistry, and biological safety analysis. Results will be prepared for conference presentations and journal submissions.
