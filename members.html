Graduate Students

Yenchiu Tsai — Ph.D. Candidate

Yenchiu received her B.A. degree in Economics from the Chinese University of Hong Kong and her master's degree in Food Science from Jiangnan University. Yenchiu Tsai is a Ph.D. Candidate in the Meat Science Laboratory whose work centers on MALDI–MSI, spatial chemistry, and metabolomic profiling of variety meats. She brings a strong analytical chemistry background and leads imaging‑based projects that support the lab’s spatial nutrition research agenda. Her doctoral work aims to establish high‑resolution chemical mapping approaches that improve understanding of nutrient distribution in organ tissues.

Guangyi Shen — Ph.D. Candidate in meat quality and metabolomics.

Guangyi Shen is a food scientist whose research interests span nutrition, food chemistry, and food safety. She received her bachelor's degree from China Agricultural University and completed her M.S. in Food Science at Purdue University, where her research focused on the relationship between diet, gut health, and inflammation. Her work explored how bioactive compounds in foods can influence the gut microbiota and contribute to improved health outcomes. As a Ph.D. student, Guangyi's research will focus on food chemical and biological safety. She is interested in applying advanced analytical instrumentation, machine learning, and artificial intelligence to detect food hazards, improve food quality assessment, and develop innovative solutions for safer and more sustainable food systems. Her goal is to combine cutting-edge technologies with food science to address emerging challenges in food safety and public health.
